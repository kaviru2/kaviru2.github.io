About

I am a final-year B.Sc. (Hons) Information Systems student at the University of Colombo School of Computing, graduating in May 2027. My research asks how AI agents can recover when the actions they take in real systems fail: compensation and rollback, testing agents in stateful environments, and what recovery looks like in deployed agent tools. I also work on concurrent software systems, automated security analysis, and AI for learning.

Until December 2026 I am in Higashi-Hiroshima, Japan, on a GS-JTI placement with SYNRA. I plan to start a PhD in late 2027 or early 2028.

Selected Projects

RAC: Agent Compensation & Rollback Library

A framework-independent compensation and rollback library for ReAct agents. It records actions and compensates for completed operations when later steps fail, helping preserve consistency in agentic workflows.

Repository

SELAR: Semantic Linking for Active Retention

A theory-grounded generative-AI reading system that surfaces argument-level links across a learner’s document library. The project includes a controlled pilot measuring retention after 24 hours and seven days.

MediQ

An AI-assisted first-aid guidance system and global finalist in the IEEE IES Generative AI Challenge 2024. My primary contribution was the design of generative user interfaces that adapt to situational context.
