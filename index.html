About

I’m a final-year undergraduate at the University of Colombo School of Computing, graduating in May 2027. I study how AI agents recover when the actions they take in real systems fail: compensation and rollback, testing agents in stateful environments, and what recovery looks like in deployed agent tools.

Previously I was a research intern at WSO2, where I worked on RAC with Srinath Perera and Frank Leymann. I’m currently in Japan on a GS‑JTI placement with SYNRA, and I plan to start a PhD in late 2027 or early 2028.
